1. 服务概述与核心隐私原则

1. Service Overview & Privacy Principles

FooCloud（福云）是一款致力于为个人与团队提供高效、安全的文件同步与云端存储解决方案。我们坚信：您的数据归您所有，且仅归您所有。

我们遵循最小必要原则（Data Minimization）与端到端隐私保护设计（Privacy by Design），绝不将您的个人数据或文件内容出售给任何第三方用于广告追踪或模型训练。

FooCloud is a high-performance cloud synchronization and storage service engineered for individuals and organizations. Our core philosophy is simple: Your data belongs to you, and only you.

We adhere to data minimization and privacy-by-design standards. We never sell your personal data, synchronization metadata, or file payloads to third parties for advertising or unauthorized AI model training.

零知识承诺 (Zero-Knowledge Stance) Zero-Knowledge Commitment

在启用了客户端专属加密模式下，FooCloud 服务端仅保存加密后的二进制数据块（Chunks）。即便发生物理介质泄漏，没有您的私钥，任何人都无法解密您的文件。

When client-side encryption is enabled, FooCloud servers only hold encrypted binary chunks. Without your private keys, nobody—including FooCloud operators—can decrypt your file contents.

3. 数据的使用方式

3. How We Use Information

我们收集的数据仅在以下特定场景中使用：

We process collected data exclusively under the following strict scenarios:

  • 核心同步服务：Core Synchronization: 比较客户端与服务器端文件的差异，以最小带宽执行分块（Delta-sync）断点续传。 Computing diffs between local directories and cloud storage to perform bandwidth-efficient delta sync.
  • 历史版本与回收站：Version History & Recovery: 根据您的套餐规则保留文件的历史修改版本，以便在误删或勒索软件攻击时还原。 Maintaining file snapshots to allow rollbacks and recovery against accidental deletions or ransomware.
  • 服务安全防护：Abuse Prevention & Security: 检测异常高频登录、暴力破解、跨地域异常网关请求等安全威胁。 Detecting anomalous login attempts, credential stuffing, and unauthorized gateway relays.

4. 加密技术与存储安全

4. Storage Security & Encryption

FooCloud 采用国际前沿的密码学标准保障数据在传输中与静态存储时的安全性：

  • 传输层安全 (In-Transit)：客户端与网关节点之间的所有通信均强制使用 TLS 1.3 / HTTPS 协议，支持 Perfect Forward Secrecy (PFS)。
  • 静态存储加密 (At-Rest)：存储于后端对象存储或磁盘的数据块采用行业标准的 AES-256 算法加密。
  • 自建私有网关支持：针对高级用户，FooCloud 允许配置自托管同步网关（Self-hosted Gateway），数据流直接流向您的私有服务器。

FooCloud implements top-tier cryptographic standards to safeguard data in transit and at rest:

  • In-Transit Protection: All socket connections and API requests utilize mandatory TLS 1.3 with Perfect Forward Secrecy (PFS).
  • At-Rest Encryption: Synchronized blocks stored in persistence layers are encrypted using industry-standard AES-256.
  • Self-Hosted Gateways: Power users can route synchronization through private self-hosted gateways directly to their own hardware.

5. 数据共享与第三方披露

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

我们严格限制数据向外部流出。除以下法律必须或基础基础设施所必需的情况外，我们不会向第三方共享任何数据：

  • 基础云基础设施服务商：如提供高可用算力与数据持久化的合规机房（服务商仅作为数据管道，无权访问解密信息）。
  • 法律合规要求：当且仅当收到合法、有效的法院令状或强制性司法要求时，在严格法律审查后提供法定范围内的信息。

We enforce strict boundary controls. We do not expose or share your data with third parties except in the following limited infrastructural and legal contexts:

  • Infrastructure Providers: Certified cloud facilities providing physical compute and block persistence without payload access permissions.
  • Legal Compliance: Only upon receipt of legally binding, verified court warrants or statutory enforcement orders.

6. 用户权利与数据删除

6. Your Rights & Data Erasure

您对存储在 FooCloud（福云）中的数据享有完整的控制权：

  • 数据导出权：您可以随时通过客户端打包同步目录，导出所有原始文件与目录结构。
  • 版本彻底清除：您可以随时清空“回收站”或特定文件的历史版本，该操作将触发物理存储块的不可逆覆写擦除。
  • 账户注销与遗忘权：在客户端或控制台申请注销账户后，系统将在 7 个自然日后永久销毁关联的所有元数据与文件块。

You maintain ultimate sovereignty over your synchronized assets on FooCloud:

  • Data Portability: Export, package, and download your entire storage hierarchy locally at any time without vendor lock-in.
  • Permanent Shredding: Emptying your trash or file revision tree triggers cryptographic deletion across all storage clusters.
  • Right to be Forgotten: Initiating account termination leads to irreversible erasure of your profile and data within 7 calendar days.
