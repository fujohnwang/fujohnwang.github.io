Good to know

A few quick answers.

Are my images ever uploaded?

No. Recognition and compositing happen locally with Apple Vision and Core Image. RMBG has no network entitlement.

Which Macs are supported?

RMBG requires macOS 14 or later.

What kind of image works best?

Images with a distinct subject, clear edges, and stronger foreground-to-background contrast usually produce the cleanest result.

What format does RMBG export?

Saved results use PNG and preserve transparency. Clipboard copies include PNG and TIFF data for broad Mac app compatibility.

How do I remove an image background on Mac?

Open RMBG, then choose, paste, or drop an image. The app removes the BG locally; review the split view and save or copy the transparent result.

What does RMBG mean?

RMBG is short for “remove background,” or simply “remove BG.” It is a focused offline BG remover for Mac, without the browser upload step used by online background removal tools.
