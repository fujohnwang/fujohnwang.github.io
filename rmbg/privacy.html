RMBG Privacy Policy

Effective: September 5, 2026

Summary: RMBG does not collect, retain, sell, or transmit personal data or images. Background removal happens entirely on the user’s Mac.

Data Processed

RMBG processes only images that the user explicitly chooses, pastes, or drops into the app. Apple Vision and Core Image perform this processing locally on the device.

Collection and Transmission

RMBG has no account system, advertising, analytics, telemetry, third-party data collection SDK, or network entitlement. Source images and results are never uploaded or sent to the developer or any third party.

Files and Clipboard

The app reads a file only after an explicit selection or drop, reads an image from the clipboard only after Paste, writes a transparent PNG to the clipboard only after Copy, and saves a PNG only to a location confirmed by the user.

Storage and Retention

RMBG creates no image database or cloud backup. Images are held only in memory as needed while the app runs. Files explicitly exported by the user remain under the user’s control.

Children and Changes

Because RMBG collects no personal data, it does not knowingly collect data from children. If application capabilities change, an updated effective date will be published on this page.

Support

For product details, visit the RMBG product page. For questions about the app or this policy, visit the RMBG support page.

RMBG 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 5 日

简要说明：RMBG 不收集、不存储、不出售，也不向任何服务器传输个人数据或图片。图片背景移除完全在用户的 Mac 上完成。

处理的数据

RMBG 只处理用户主动选择、粘贴或拖拽到应用中的图片。处理使用 macOS 内置的 Apple Vision 与 Core Image，在设备本地完成。

数据收集与传输

RMBG 不包含账号系统、广告、分析、遥测或第三方数据收集 SDK，也没有网络访问权限。源图片和处理结果不会被上传或发送给开发者及任何第三方。

文件和剪贴板访问

应用仅在用户明确选择或拖拽时读取对应文件，仅在用户执行“粘贴”时读取剪贴板图片，仅在用户执行“复制”时写入透明 PNG，并仅在用户确认保存位置后写入导出文件。

存储与保留

RMBG 不建立图片数据库或云端备份。处理中的图片只保留在应用运行所需的内存中。用户主动保存的输出文件由用户自行管理。

儿童隐私与政策变更

由于 RMBG 不收集任何个人数据，因此不会有意收集儿童的数据。如本政策随应用能力变化而更新，新版本会在本页面公布生效日期。

支持

有关产品详情，请访问 RMBG 产品主页。有关应用使用或本政策的问题，请访问 RMBG 支持页面。
